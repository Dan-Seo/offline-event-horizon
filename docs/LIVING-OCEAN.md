# Living ocean implementation

MirrorSea has a curved seabed authored 51–77 artistic metres beneath its existing spherical water cap. Rendering and recovery consume the same indexed Float32 polar mesh in `ocean-depth.ts`; recovery samples its triangles barycentrically, not the analytic authoring function. One continuous floor supplies both the broad cap and the 1,600-metre local garden, avoiding overlapping floor meshes. The garden has restrained sand ripples, low reef rocks, curved tapered blades in 40 clusters, three quiet schools and suspended motes. Nacre reuses its existing triangle terrain as lagoon seabed in the unchanged regional frame; terrain, coast, walking and PILGRIM water contacts are preserved.

Free flight crosses water continuously. Orpheus recovery solves the curved seabed plus 3 metres along the radial direction within the 30,000-metre northern cap. Nacre solves existing ground triangles plus 2 metres inside its lagoon. These are physical safety queries, never planner inputs. `floorClearance` reports signed radial distance in metres above that permitted flight floor (zero at recovery), while legacy `seabedHeight` remains in each region's local units. Surface-wave excursions are excluded from signed mean depth.

Regional activation requires the northern hemisphere of its existing local frame (`local.y + 1 > 0`) and a finite local domain. Regional underwater inspection additionally requires the 0.152-radius lagoon footprint. Opposite-hemisphere and out-of-domain updates clear stale water depth/submersion; the engine receives no regional ocean outside that footprint.

Water uses a 0.02 normal-incidence Schlick Fresnel term and strong grazing reflection. Above water, per-vertex water-column depths drive scalar artistic transmission `exp(-0.085 * depth / max(0.15, abs(viewCosine)))`. Surface opacity is `F + (1-F)(1-T)`; its source colour is normalized by opacity so alpha blending applies Fresnel reflection once. Deep floor contribution is below 1.4% at 51 m and 0.2% at 77 m even at normal incidence; a 1 m shallow column retains about 92% transmission before Fresnel. Absorbed light supplies dark turquoise scattering. This column/slant approximation is not refracted ray tracing or RGB transmission through arbitrary foreground objects.

The depth-writing surface bounds the underwater upward view. Underwater, the beauty pipeline decodes existing logarithmic depth, converts axial depth to ray distance, and applies RGB Beer–Lambert-inspired absorption and depth-dependent scattering once, before tone mapping. A uniform branch skips that whole-view compositor above water; above-water attenuation comes from the surface material instead. Air haze is disabled while submerged to avoid stacking it with the water treatment. Sensors do not use this compositor; their idealized visible water surface and reflective-water failure semantics remain unchanged.

The animated light lattice is **artistic caustics**, not a refracted photon solution. Refraction concentrates light in real water; this cheap pattern is motivated by [GPU Gems, Rendering Water Caustics](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-2-rendering-water-caustics). Full fluid dynamics, radiative transfer, volumetric shafts, water-path segmentation through above-water objects, and biological ecosystems are absent. Local life is authored and bounded, not a global ecological simulation. Transparent objects inherit the nearest depth-writing surface's optical distance.

Per ocean maxima: 120 low rocks, 700 blades, 72 fish, 160 motes, four instanced draws plus floor/water. Quality fractions are 1/0.8/0.55/0.3 for Ultra/High/Balanced/Battery. Only fish matrices update on CPU, at most 72 per visible ocean per changed simulation timestamp; other animation uses the shared simulation time. Hidden ocean groups skip updates. No new render targets or sensor passes are introduced. Regional oceans are constructed only for Nacre. Pause freezes waves, blades, schools, particles and caustics.

Integration: the Orpheus visual globe must be recessed at least 82 radial units within the 30,000 northern cap, blending outward; water cap radius is 46,000 and sphere radius 48,000. Cosmic owner supplies that underlay/world-north alignment. Engine exposes `ocean.sanctuary`, `ocean.regional`, and `cosmic` in existing read-only inspection. A future optional quiet destination can use world position `(0,-34,460)` looking toward `(400,-40,0)`; no shared destination was added.

Repair evidence: closest-point evaluation of every actual floor triangle whose horizontal projection intersects the 30 km cap bounds the deepest radial seabed at **76.8336371 m**, including triangle curvature sag. The extremum is inside the cap at approximately `(-522.2534, -86.0541, 781.6075)`. The cosmic minimum recess of 89.144 m leaves over 12.31 m clearance; no cosmic constant change is required. The previously reported `j=103, i=168` centroid now recovers 3 radial metres above its rendered triangle. Independent Three ray/triangle intersections validate the sampler at 32 azimuths just inside the cap edge.

Manual QA route: open `/`, enter the experience, select **Fly** if riding PILGRIM, stay at initial Last Light, and hold **X** to descend; **Space** ascends in the current camera frame. At the initial orientation, a few seconds reaches the surface and another few reaches the garden. Use **WASD**, mouse look and Control for precision. Look down for sand/reef, sideways for blades/schools, up for the lit surface; ascend until the original sky returns. For regional QA, select **Nacre**, **Approach**, use **Fly** if walking/riding, fly toward the lagoon centre and descend. No swim mode is introduced.

Validation: `npm run typecheck`; `node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/ocean-depth.test.ts tests/ocean-render.test.ts` checks actual MirrorSea geometry and flight recovery, cap-edge raycasts and radial-depth bound, actual Nacre approach activation/stale-depth reset, transmission limits, tapered blade geometry, quality counts, curvature and both radial floors.
