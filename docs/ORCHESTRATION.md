# VASTNESS 3.0 orchestration

These rules govern this release pass and subsequent project work. The final optimization rules take precedence over earlier mission wording and the global “MAX final review only” convention at the listed gates. They do not claim a current release has passed any gate.

## Gate authority

MAX is principal architect and release authority at:

- **A:** architecture, contracts, ownership, and task graph.
- **B:** sensor truth/estimate and perception/map/planner boundaries.
- **C:** vehicle/world/controller and render/perception/lifecycle integration.
- **D:** personal browser experience, visual restraint, and manual takeover.
- **E:** evidence and production acceptance.

Between gates, delegate implementation, QA, and documentation. Do not micromanage or perform prestige rewrites of accepted work. Root retains release ownership and the two-layer healing/research experience.

## Dispatch and isolation

Choose the cheapest reliable available worker likely to finish in one or two serious attempts: Luna low for repetitive work, Terra medium for normal work, Terra low for dispatch, and Astra low for difficult code likely otherwise to need Sol high. Use MAX only for strategic uncertainty and gate authority. Confirm actual model, effort, permissions, context, isolation, local instructions/skills, and version documentation; never imply unavailable configuration.

Every compact context-firewall packet states: objective/background; owned files; read-only dependencies; interface; constraints; tests; and return artifact. One active writer owns each surface; reading may be shared. Worktrees are allowed only with an intentional merge plan; serialize engine, state, input, config, and render-loop work. Start at roughly two substantial workers on the measured PC and account for other projects/RAM; expand toward two to five only when independent work and headroom justify it. Avoid ceremonial fanout and recursive delegation.

Workers return changed files, design decisions, test commands/results, measured behavior, unresolved problems, assumptions, and integration notes. Communicate concise decisions, assignments, evidence, blockers, and results; do not expose private chains of thought.

## Review, repair, and acceptance

Critical dynamics, transforms, VO, sensors, ATE/RPE, map/navigation, coupling, WebGPU, and synchronization work requires an independent reviewer, never implementer self-certification. Review returns **PASS**, **PASS WITH ISSUES**, or **REJECT**, with consequential findings, evidence/functions, and uncertainty. MAX reads material-risk, cross-system, mathematical, truth-boundary, performance, or disputed changes only.

The reviewer receives the requirement, implementation diff, tests, and evidence. Accept contract-compliant work unless consequential architectural, numerical, performance, safety, or experience defects remain; repair those defects without speculative rewrites. Root must not reread every mechanically safe diff.

Escalate implementation → self-fix → independent review → one targeted repair → MAX with accumulated evidence. Use proportional tests and do not repeat a passed test without a new reason. Prefer source evidence to assertions. At release, follow the mission’s cycle, including deployed PILGRIM and lab retesting after Vercel deployment, and report actual limitations and measured evidence.
